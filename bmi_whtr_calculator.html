BMI + Waist-to-Height (WHtR) Risk Calculator

This calculator includes standard BMI and the waist-to-height ratio (WHtR), based on research highlighted by ScienceAlert and the supporting study in The Journal of Nutrition (DOI: 10.1016/j.tjnut.2026.101426).

For adults and educational screening only — not a diagnosis.

How results are interpreted

  • BMI categories (WHO): Underweight < 18.5, Healthy 18.5-24.9, Overweight 25.0-29.9, Obesity ≥ 30.
  • WHtR cut points (research-linked):
    • Male: normal < 0.50, high fat ≥ 0.50, excess fat ≥ 0.53
    • Female: normal < 0.51, high fat ≥ 0.51, excess fat ≥ 0.54

The supporting NHANES-based study reported WHtR-assessed high/excess fat categories were more strongly associated with hypertension than BMI categories.
